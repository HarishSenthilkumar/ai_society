# AI Society

AI Society is a simulation that looks at how a group of simple AI agents interact when they are given personalities.

## Research Question

What happens when people in a society make their own decisions based on their needs, and goals?

## About

The simulation creates a society of AI agents. Each agent has its own:

- Hunger, energy, health, and happiness
- Personality
- Job and money
- Relationships
- Goals and needs

Agents make decisions throughout the simulation. They can work, eat, sleep, shop, socialize, form relationships, and more.

## How It Works

Each agent chooses what to do based on what it needs at the time.

The simulation also includes:

- Jobs and money
- Food and markets
- Relationships
- Families
- Births and deaths
- Random events
- Changes in food prices

The simulation runs for a long period of time so I can see how the society changes.

## Goal

The main goal is to see how simple decisions made by agents can lead to larger patterns in a society.
